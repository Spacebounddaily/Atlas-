# 1630-1776 Colonial Lineage Arc

## Introduction
This document outlines the colonial lineage arc from 1630 to 1776, focusing on the key events, figures, and developments in the early American colonies.

## Key Events
- **1630:** Founding of the Massachusetts Bay Colony.
- **1640s:** The Great Migration of Puritans to New England.
- **1664:** New Amsterdam becomes New York, following British conquest.
- **1676:** Bacon's Rebellion in Virginia, showcasing tensions between colonists and Native Americans.
- **1700s:** Expansion of colonies and increasing British oversight.
- **1754-1763:** French and Indian War, changing the territorial landscape.
- **1765:** Stamp Act – taxation without representation leads to colonial unrest.

## Key Figures
- **John Winthrop:** First governor of the Massachusetts Bay Colony.
- **William Penn:** Founder of Pennsylvania, promoting religious freedom.
- **Benjamin Franklin:** Influential in colonial affairs and a key figure in the push for independence.
- **Thomas Jefferson:** A leader in the movement toward independence later in the century.

## Conclusion
The colonial period from 1630 to 1776 laid the groundwork for the formation of the United States, marked by struggle for independence, cultural development, and conflict with colonial powers.
